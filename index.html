Wave Properties & Phase ｜波的基本性質與相位

Definition Formula Explanation Calculation

Core Concepts & Wave Types (核心概念與波的種類)

  • Wave: A form of energy transfer via oscillation without transferring matter. Most waves require a medium, except electromagnetic (EM) waves (which can travel through a vacuum). 波是透過振動傳遞能量的形式，過程中不會傳遞物質。除電磁波（EM waves）外，大多數波需要介質傳播。
  • Transverse Waves: Particle oscillation is perpendicular ($\perp$) to the direction of wave travel (e.g., EM waves, water waves, waves on a string). 橫波：質點振動方向與波的傳播方向垂直（例：電磁波、水波、弦線波）。
  • Longitudinal Waves: Particle oscillation is parallel ($\parallel$) to the direction of wave travel (e.g., sound waves). 縱波：質點振動方向與波的傳播方向平行（例：聲波）。
  • Wavefront: A line/surface joining all points in a wave that are at the same phase (e.g., all peaks at $90^\circ$). Waves always travel perpendicular ($\perp$) to wavefronts. 波前：波中所有處於相同相位的點連成的線（如所有波峰）。波的傳播方向永遠與波前垂直。

Key Quantities, Units & Formulas (物理量、單位與公式)

  • Displacement $s$[m]: The distance and direction of a particle from its equilibrium position. 位移 $s$：質點偏離其平衡位置的距離及方向。
  • Amplitude $A$[m]: The maximum displacement of a particle from its equilibrium position. 振幅 $A$：質點偏離平衡位置的最大位移。
  • Wavelength $\lambda$[m]: The distance between one point on a wave and the next consecutive point in the same phase (e.g., crest to crest). 波長 $\lambda$：波上相鄰兩個相同相位點之間的距離（如相鄰波峰間距）。
  • Period $T$[s] & Frequency $f$[Hz]: Time for one complete oscillation ($T$); number of complete wave cycles per second ($f$). 週期 $T$（完成一次全振動的時間）與頻率 $f$（每秒完成的完整波週期數）。
  • Wave Speed $v$[m s⁻¹]: Rate of movement of the wave profile.

Principle of Superposition, Interference & Beats ｜波的疊加原理、干涉與拍頻

Concept Explanation Calculation

Superposition & Interference (疊加與干涉)

  • Principle of Superposition: When two or more waves meet at a point, the resultant displacement is the vector sum of the individual displacements ($s = s_1 + s_2$). After passing through each other, the waves continue unchanged in their original forms. 波的疊加原理：當兩個或多個波相遇時，該點的合位移等於各波單獨引起的位移的向量和（$s = s_1 + s_2$）。兩波穿過彼此後會恢復原本形狀繼續傳播。
  • Constructive Interference: Occurs when waves arrive in phase (phase difference = $0^\circ, 360^\circ, \dots$ or path difference = $n\lambda$), producing maximum resultant amplitude ($A_1 + A_2$). 相長干涉：當兩波同相相遇（相位差 $0^\circ$ 或路程差 $n\lambda$），振幅相加達最大值。
  • Destructive Interference: Occurs when waves arrive out of phase. If identical waves are $180^\circ$ ($\pi\text{ rad}$) out of phase (path difference = $(n + \frac{1}{2})\lambda$), they cancel completely to give zero amplitude. 相消干涉：當兩波反相相遇。若兩個相同波的相位差為 $180^\circ$（$\pi\text{ rad}$），則完全抵消成零振幅。

Beats (Superposition of Different Frequencies) (拍頻現象)

  • Formation of Beats: When two continuous waves of slightly different frequencies ($f_1$ and $f_2$) superpose, their phase relationship changes continuously over time, causing alternating regions of constructive interference (loud/large amplitude) and destructive interference (quiet/small amplitude). 拍的形成：當兩個頻率略為不同的波疊加時，相位關係隨時間不斷變化，導致相長干涉與相消干涉交替出現。
  • Notes Example (p.4): Combining a $40\text{ Hz}$ wave and a $50\text{ Hz}$ wave produces a combined wave with a beat frequency of $10\text{ Hz}$. 筆記例子：$40\text{ Hz}$ 與 $50\text{ Hz}$ 兩波疊加，產生 $10\text{ Hz}$ 的拍頻。
fbeat = |f1 − f2|

Standing (Stationary) Waves & Harmonics ｜駐波與諧波模式

Concept Explanation Diagram Calculation

Formation Criteria & Structure (形成條件與結構)

  • Definition: Formed when two continuous progressive waves travelling in opposite directions superpose continuously. Unlike progressive waves, a standing wave does not transfer net energy. 駐波：由兩個沿相反方向傳播的連續行進波不斷疊加而成，不會淨傳遞能量。
  • 3 Required Criteria: (1) Same speed $v$ and frequency $f$, (2) Similar amplitude, (3) Constant phase relationship (coherent). 三個必要條件：(1) 相同波速 $v$ 與頻率 $f$；(2) 相近振幅；(3) 恆定相位關係（即相干波 coherent）。
  • Nodes (N) & Antinodes (A): Nodes never move (zero amplitude; closed ends must be Nodes). Antinodes oscillate with maximum amplitude (open ends must be Antinodes). Distance between adjacent N–N or A–A = $\frac{\lambda}{2}$; adjacent N–A = $\frac{\lambda}{4}$. 波節 (N) 永不振動（閉口端必為 N）；波腹 (A) 振幅最大（開口端必為 A）。相鄰 N–N 或 A–A 距離為 $\lambda/2$；相鄰 N–A 為 $\lambda/4$。

Waves on a String (Close–Close) (弦線上的駐波公式)

  • Wave Speed on a String: Depends on string tension $T$ [N] and mass per unit length $\mu$ [kg m⁻¹]. 弦線波速取決於張力 $T$ 與單位長度質量（線密度）$\mu$。
  • Fundamental Frequency ($1^{\text{st}}$ Harmonic): Since $\lambda_1 = 2L$ for a close–close string of length $L$:
v = √Tμ
f = 1λ √Tμ
f0 = 12L √Tμ

Diffraction, Slit Patterns, Grating & Huygens' Principle ｜繞射、干涉條紋、繞射光柵與惠更斯原理

Concept Explanation Diagram Formula

Diffraction & Gap Size Rules (繞射與縫隙大小關係)

  • Diffraction: The spreading of wave energy behind/around the edges of an obstacle or after passing through a gap. 繞射：波經過障礙物邊緣或狹縫後，波的能量向四周擴散的現象。
  • Gap ≈ λ (Comparable): Diffraction spreading is most significant with strong transmitted energy. 縫寬 ≈ λ：繞射現象最顯著，且能傳遞足夠能量。
  • Gap ≫ λ (Much larger): Diffraction effect is negligible / not significant; wave travels mostly in a straight line. 縫寬 ≫ λ：繞射不明顯，波幾乎沿直線前進。
  • Gap ≪ λ (Much smaller): Wide semicircular spreading occurs, but only a tiny amount of energy passes through. 縫寬 ≪ λ：雖有明顯擴散，但僅極少能量能穿過狹縫。

Single Slit, Double Slit & Diffraction Grating (單縫、雙縫與繞射光柵)

  • Single Slit: Forms a wide, bright central maximum, with much dimmer, narrower bright fringes on either side. 單縫繞射：形成寬而亮的中央亮紋，兩側亮紋隨距離急劇變暗。
  • Double Slit: Two coherent diffracted waves superpose, producing many equally spaced bright maxima and dark minima. 雙縫干涉：兩個相干繞射波疊加，形成多條等距明暗相間的干涉條紋。
  • Diffraction Grating: Contains a very large number of parallel slits (labelled by grating density, e.g., $400\text{ lines per cm}$), producing very sharp, bright, widely separated maxima. 繞射光柵：具有極多平行狹縫（如 $400\text{ 條/cm}$），產生極銳利、明亮且分得很開的亮紋。
nλ = d sinθ
d = 1grating density

*Unit Trap: If grating density = $400\text{ cm}^{-1}$, convert to $\text{m}^{-1}$ first: $d = \frac{0.01\text{ m}}{400} = 2.5 \times 10^{-5}\text{ m}$.

Huygens' Principle (惠更斯原理 · Notes p.12–13)

Statement: Every point on a wavefront can be considered as a point source of secondary spherical wavelets spreading forward at the wave speed. These spherical wavelets superpose (constructively along their common tangent envelope, destructively elsewhere) to form the new wavefront. 惠更斯原理：波前上的每一點皆可視為產生次級球面子波（secondary spherical wavelets）的點波源。這些子波互相疊加（在切線包絡面上相長干涉，其餘方向相消），從而形成新的波前。

✍️ Exam Mark-Scheme Template: Explain why we can hear someone talking inside a room, but cannot see them around the doorway (Notes p.10)
1M
A typical doorway width is about $1\text{ m}$. Human speech ($f \approx 330\text{ Hz}$) has a wavelength of $\lambda \approx 1.1\text{ m}$, which is comparable to the doorway width. （門口寬度約 $1\text{ m}$，人聲波長約 $1.1\text{ m}$，與門口寬度相若。）
1M
Therefore, sound waves diffract significantly around the doorway edge, allowing us to hear the speaker without a direct line of sight. （因此聲波在門口發生顯著繞射，使我們能聽到聲音。）
1M
In contrast, visible light has a very short wavelength ($\lambda \sim 10^{-7}\text{ m} \ll 1\text{ m}$), so light undergoes negligible diffraction through the doorway and travels in straight lines. （相反，可見光波長極短（約 $10^{-7}\text{ m}$），遠小於門口寬度，繞射不明顯，故無法看到對方。）
